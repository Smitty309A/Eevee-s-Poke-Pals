Our Story

Welcome to Eeveespokepals! What started as a fun family hobby turned into a full-blown mission to catch 'em all. We collect, open, and hunt for Pokémon cards together, sharing the thrill of the chase with collectors of all ages. Whether you are a lifelong fan or just starting your journey, we’re glad you're part of our crew!

Hiding Free Pokémon Packs Across Barrie Every Single Day!

We love giving back to our awesome local community! To spread the Pokémon joy, we provide free Pokémon packs to the public in random hiding places across the city of Barrie every single day. Want to find today's stash? Watch our latest videos—we tuck hidden location hints into every single upload so you can track them down!
